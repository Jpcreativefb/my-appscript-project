# v1.2.17o — Appearance Studio Team Canvas Repair

Replaces the team-button grid with a true layered canvas. Images can float anywhere across the full button or fill the entire button as a background. City + team name are one independent text layer, scores are an independent layer with inline/center/corner anchors and X/Y offsets, Home/Away side controls move both city and team text, the scoreboard/status strip gains background/opacity/border/height/radius/font-size controls, and the admin preview uses document-level sticky behavior instead of a nested vertical scroll box.
